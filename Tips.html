Tips for New Players

Start with a few simple habits. You do not need to learn everything in one day. Keep practising and think about what went well in each match.

During a Match

  • Keep your crosshair near head level so you need less movement when aiming.
  • Check corners before walking into a new area.
  • Listen for footsteps. They can help you notice nearby players.
  • Tell your team where you see enemies. Keep the information short and clear.
  • Use cover and avoid standing in the middle of an open area.

A Simple Practice Plan

  1. Choose one map and learn its main paths.
  2. Practise aiming with one weapon.
  3. Learn how to use a smoke grenade.
  4. Play a match and choose one thing to improve next time.

Be a Good Teammate

Stay calm when you lose a round. Listen to your teammates and avoid arguing. Everyone makes mistakes while learning.
