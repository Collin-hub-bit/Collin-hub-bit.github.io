CS2 Weapons

Different weapons are useful in different situations. Here are three weapons that players can buy. Try one at a time while learning the game.

AK-47

The AK-47 is a rifle bought by the Terrorist team. Its recoil takes practice to control. Start by practising short bursts before trying longer sprays.

M4A1-S

The M4A1-S is a rifle bought by the Counter-Terrorist team. It has a silencer. Players can use it to watch paths and defend bombsites.

AWP

The AWP is a sniper rifle with a scope. It is useful for watching long paths. Aim carefully and have a safe place to move after a shot.

Weapon Type Who Can Buy It?
AK-47 Rifle Terrorists
M4A1-S Rifle Counter-Terrorists
AWP Sniper rifle Both teams

Other Equipment

Weapons are only part of a player's equipment. Armour gives protection, and grenades can help a team move or defend an area. Smoke grenades block vision. Players can also pick up weapons dropped by other players.
