About CS2

CS2 is a team-based shooting game. Each player sees the game through their character's eyes. Players use weapons, grenades, and teamwork to complete their goals.

In bomb-defusal matches, the two teams are called Terrorists and Counter-Terrorists. They have different jobs during a round.

Terrorists

The Terrorists try to plant the bomb at one of the bombsites. After planting it, they protect the area and try to stop the other team from defusing it.

Counter-Terrorists

The Counter-Terrorists defend the bombsites and try to stop the bomb plant. If the bomb is planted, they try to reach it safely and defuse it.

Buying Equipment

Players use in-game money to buy weapons, armour, and grenades. Think about what you and your team need before spending it.

Working Together

Tell your teammates where you see enemies. Listen to their information too. A simple plan is easier to follow when everyone understands it.
