CS2 Maps

Maps are the places where matches happen. Each map has different paths, open areas, and places to take cover. Learning a map helps you understand where to go.

Dust II

Dust II is a classic map with tunnels and open areas. Some paths are useful for long-range shots. Learn where the tunnels lead and use cover when crossing open ground.

Nuke

Nuke is set in a power plant. Its bombsites are on different levels. Players need to learn the stairs, ramps, and other routes between the two sites.

Overpass

Overpass has a park, a canal, and city streets. Some paths are open, while others are narrow. Learn the routes between the bombsites so you can help your team.

What to Learn First

  • Find Bombsite A and Bombsite B.
  • Learn the main paths from your starting area.
  • Learn place names so you can give clear information.
