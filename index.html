Welcome to Our CS2 Website

Counter-Strike 2 is an online shooting game made by Valve. Players use weapons and teamwork to win rounds.

This website gives a simple introduction to the game. You can learn the basic rules, look at maps, and compare weapons.

There is also a page with tips for new players. If you are new to CS2, the About page is a good place to start.

More Than Just Aiming

Good aim helps, but players also need to think about their next move. One player can watch a path while another covers a bombsite. Sharing information can help the whole team make a better plan.
